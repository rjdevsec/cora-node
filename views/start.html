How a run works

  1. Assets from the asset register: categorisation and ownership
  2. Patch compliance from the vulnerability scanner
  3. Anti-malware agent status
  4. Privileged accounts in PAM, application and database servers
  5. Logs flowing to the SIEM
  6. Exceptions raised for these assets
  7. Open application and asset risks in the risk register
  8. Backups scheduled, protected and restore-tested
  9. Latest DR test results
  10. Business impact of the risks found
  11. Plausible scenarios generated from the risks, then all risks with scores
